# Corpse obsession

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: major mood break. DLC: none.

The pawn goes to a grave or sarcophagus, digs up a corpse and drops it on a busy spot, like a dining table. Then the break ends.

Needs a filled grave or sarcophagus, and the pawn must be able to use its hands (manipulation).

Quick, but everyone who sees the corpse gets "Observed corpse" mood penalties, and a colonist's corpse gives the whole colony "Colonist left unburied".
