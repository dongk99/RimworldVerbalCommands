# Entity liberator

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: extreme mood break, anomalous. DLC: Anomaly.

The pawn is drawn to a captive entity and frees it: it walks to a random contained entity it can reach and releases it.

Ends once an entity is released, or after enough time.

On recovery the pawn gets Void catharsis (+30) instead of the normal Catharsis.
