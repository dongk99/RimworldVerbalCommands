# Social fighting

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: special state, not from mood. DLC: none.

Starts when one pawn slights or insults another (far more likely from an insult) and the other gets angry.

Very short, usually minor injuries, but can end with a pawn downed, scarred or rarely killed. Ends after a short time or when one fighter is downed or killed.
