# Fire starting spree

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: extreme mood break. DLC: none.

The pawn sets flammable things on fire, and may go for volatile things like chemfuel.

Only pyromaniacs get it. For them it replaces every extreme break, and it also happens at random regardless of mood (wiki: MTB 50 days; no Catharsis after those random ones). The pyrophobia gene (Biotech) switches the pyromaniac trait off, this break included.

Short. Having another colonist follow the pyromaniac and put the fires out is enough.
