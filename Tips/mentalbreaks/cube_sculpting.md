# Cube sculpting

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: special state, not from mood. DLC: Anomaly.

Pawns with interest in the golden cube build a cube-shaped sculpture on the ground (sand, dirt or scrap, depending on the surface). Then the break ends.

The sculpture has quality, so a pawn with Inspired creativity may spend the inspiration on it.
