# Crying

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: minor mood break (babies). DLC: Biotech.

A baby cries while awake; more likely when its mood is under 50%. Silent: the only sign is the name turning green for a while.

Colonists nearby get "Baby crying" -4 mood (not psychopaths). Every pawn's opinion of the baby drops by 12.
