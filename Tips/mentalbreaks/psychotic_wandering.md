# Psychotic wandering (daze)

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: major mood break. DLC: none.

The pawn wanders around aimlessly and recovers on its own. The letter calls it a "Daze".

It can ignore food restrictions. A dazed colonist can be arrested if it walks into danger.

A prisoner in this state can stage a prison break: it still wanders, but can open doors, and may attack colonists in melee.

The game has two versions (normal and short) that act the same; the short one can end much sooner.

The wiki notes (unverified) that the recovery timer seems to pause while a dazed colonist sleeps.
