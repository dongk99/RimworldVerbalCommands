# Manhunter

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: special state, animals. DLC: none.

An animal hunts and attacks humans and mechanoids, like berserk. Animals maddened by an animal pulser attack mechs too, but leave tamed animals alone.

They do not break through obstacles on their own, but attack doors they saw someone go through.

Causes: mad animal event, psychic wave, psychic animal pulser, manhunter pulse, being hurt by a human (chance by the animal, the attacker's hunting stealth and distance; some herd animals join in), a failed taming attempt, or the death of its bonded master.

Ends after some time, when the animal sleeps, or when it is downed.
