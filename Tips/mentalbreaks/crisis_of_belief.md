# Crisis of belief

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: extreme mood break. DLC: Ideology.

The pawn loses 50% of its certainty in its ideoligion (not changed by Global Certainty Loss Factor).

If that takes certainty to 0%, it converts at once to another ideoligion in the world, picked at random, favouring ones with more allied factions and more pawns around. Never its current one; it can be yours.
