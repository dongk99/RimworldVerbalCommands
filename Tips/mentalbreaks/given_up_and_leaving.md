# Given up and leaving

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: extreme mood break. DLC: none.

The pawn has had enough and walks off the map, leaving the colony for good.

Counter it by arresting the pawn or downing it. A pawn may change its mind before it reaches the edge, for example inside the labyrinth (Anomaly), which it cannot leave.

Quest lodgers and slaves never get it. It never happens with seven or fewer colonists.
