# Catatonic breakdown

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: extreme mood break. DLC: none.

The pawn collapses and is incapacitated; it needs to be rescued to a bed.

Hurts nobody and breaks nothing, but the pawn is down for a long time and does no work, which hurts if it holds a vital job.

It is a health condition: healer mech serum, or unnatural healing (Anomaly), ends it at once.

Only colonists get it, including temporary ones from quests or permits. No Tortured artist inspiration after it.
