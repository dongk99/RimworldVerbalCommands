# Run wild

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: extreme mood break. DLC: none.

The pawn runs off and lives like an animal: it leaves your faction and eats wild food (berry bushes, agave). It may walk over spike traps and set them off.

It does not end by itself. Another colonist has to tame it back (like an animal of 75% wildness, Animals skill 7 or more).

Downing it is risky: it is no longer in your faction, so it can die on being downed. A psychic shock lance downs it without that risk but can damage its brain. If rescued it may join on recovery.

After taming it gets the normal Catharsis, Beauty and Comfort set to 50% and Outdoors to 100%.

Cannot start during toxic fallout or acidic smog. (1.6 code, MentalBreakWorker_RunWild: also needs the season to be livable for the pawn, and the pawn spawned, not downed, player-controlled.) No Tortured artist inspiration after it.
