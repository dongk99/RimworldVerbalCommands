# Murderous rage

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: extreme mood break. DLC: none.

The pawn picks a random colonist or prisoner and melee-attacks them until they are dead. The break ends with the kill.

Stop it by arresting or downing the pawn. A failed arrest turns it berserk, which is easier: a berserk pawn does not try to kill.

If the target is rescued or cannot be reached, the pawn picks a new victim.

Pawns lent to you by quests or permits can get it too.
