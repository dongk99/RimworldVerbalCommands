# Manhunter (blood rain)

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: special state, animals. DLC: Anomaly.

Manhunter from built-up blood rage during blood rain. Works like the normal manhunter.

Ends some time after the blood rain stops; does not end when the animal sleeps, is downed or collapses from exhaustion.
