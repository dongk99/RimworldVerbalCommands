# Fleeing in panic

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: special state, not from mood. DLC: none.

Happens to a group of non-colonist humans (raiders, traders...) once half of them (rounded up) are killed.

They run for the nearest map edge, ignoring hostiles on the way, and bash doors even when an open path is next to them. Ends when they leave the map or die.
