# Berserk

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: extreme mood break. DLC: none.

The pawn loses control and melee-attacks any living creature near it. It does not try to kill: once a target is down it moves to the next, though an unlucky hit can still kill.

Pawns incapable of violence can still go berserk. A berserk colonist uses forbidden doors and leaves doors open behind it; a berserk prisoner bashes doors until they break.

Ends when the pawn is downed. Fists or a blunt weapon are the safest way. No arrest is needed after; the pawn can be rescued right away. If it is not in danger and can be avoided, letting it wear off avoids injuries.

A failed arrest during another break can also turn the pawn berserk.
