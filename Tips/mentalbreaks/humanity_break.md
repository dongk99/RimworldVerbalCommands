# Humanity break

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: major mood break. DLC: Anomaly and Ideology.

Takes the place of any mood break for colonists whose ideoligion has the Inhumanizing: Required precept.

The pawn wanders around for a while. After recovering it gets the permanent Inhumanized condition (which stops this break from happening again, among other things) and a permanent +8 thought instead of Catharsis.
