# Confusion

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: special state, not from mood. DLC: none.

Wandering like sad wander, for pawns with Alzheimer's or dementia, or still woozy from anesthesia. Also animals whose bonded master died.

Confused pawns can wander into danger but do not strip their gear. All normal thoughts are blocked meanwhile. Ends after some time, or when the pawn is downed or killed.
