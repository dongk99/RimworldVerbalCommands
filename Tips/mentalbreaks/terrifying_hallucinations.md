# Terrifying hallucinations

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: extreme mood break, anomalous. DLC: Anomaly.

The pawn sees fellow colonists as nightmare figures and flees from anyone it takes for a threat.

Shadowy figures swirl around it and it rambles nonsense every 10-30 seconds; other colonists are slightly upset by it. The break may end when the pawn sleeps.

On recovery the pawn gets Void catharsis (+30) instead of the normal Catharsis.
