# Hard drug binge

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: extreme mood break. DLC: none.

The pawn binges on hard drugs (flake, yayo, go-juice, wake-up).

Much more dangerous than a social binge: overdose (can kill), addiction and chemical damage.

Ends when the pawn goes to bed or after some time.

Teetotalers never get it. Pawns whose ideoligion forbids drugs still can, and then get that precept's drug-use mood penalty.
