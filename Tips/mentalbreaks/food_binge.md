# Food binge

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: minor mood break. DLC: none.

The pawn eats whatever food it can get its hands on.

It ignores food policy and forbidden items, and tends to go for the best food first. If storage has no food, or only low-quality food, it may walk far across the map for other food, including insect jelly guarded by insectoids, which can get it killed.

Can only start when the pawn's map has more than 10 nutrition of human-edible food in a stockpile zone. A binge that has started does not stop when the food runs out.

Gourmand pawns also get this break outside of mood breaks (wiki: on average once every 50 days).

Ends when the pawn goes to bed or after some time. If wake-up is available the pawn takes it during the binge, which keeps the binge going to its longest length.
