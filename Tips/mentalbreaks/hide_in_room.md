# Hide in room

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: minor mood break. DLC: none.

The pawn stays in its own room, refusing to come out or take care of its needs.

Can only happen if the pawn has an assigned bedroom.

Short, and usually does not put the pawn in harm's way; at worst mild hunger or tiredness.
