# Targeted insulting spree

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: minor mood break. DLC: none.

The pawn follows one colonist and insults it over and over.

Can wreck the victim's mood: up to -33 from 10 stacked insults, which can push the victim into a break too.

An insult can start a social fight, which ends the break.
