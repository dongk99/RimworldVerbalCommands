# Targeted tantrum

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: major mood break. DLC: none.

The pawn picks one structure or item stack and punches it until it is destroyed, then picks another if that one is gone or out of reach.

Much more destructive than a plain tantrum: things actually get destroyed. Dangerous if it picks mortar shells (an antigrain warhead explodes) or stacks the colony depends on, like components or medicine.
