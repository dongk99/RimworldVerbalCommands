# Mental breaks: what every break shares

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

During a break the pawn's name shows in green on the colonist bar and you have no control over it. It may walk
into restricted areas, eat restricted food or drugs, attack allies or break things. A lightning bolt over its
portrait is yellow for a non-aggressive break (food binge, daze) and red for an aggressive one (tantrum, berserk).

Most breaks put the pawn in a mental state. A few do not: catatonic breakdown, run wild, crisis of belief.

Ending a break early:

- Arrest: by the arresting pawn's arrest success chance, the pawn either comes quietly to the nearest free
  prison bed (the break ends at once) or goes berserk. It becomes a prisoner; released, it rejoins with
  "Was imprisoned" -6 instead of Catharsis.
- Down it: blunt melee or brain shock. The only way to stop a berserk pawn. The beaten pawn thinks less of
  whoever beat it, and an already hurt pawn can die. Does not work well on pawns that feel less pain
  (painstopper, go-juice).
- Word of serenity (Royalty psycast): ends any break by putting the pawn in a 6-hour coma. Not on babies or
  children (Biotech).

After a break the pawn goes back to its schedule and work. Its other thoughts stay. It gets Catharsis (+40,
stacks up to 5 with less each time, 3 days), which usually stops a chain of breaks. Anomalous breaks (dark
visions, insane ramblings, entity slayer, entity liberator, terrifying hallucinations) give Void catharsis
(+30) instead. Tortured artists get a 50% chance of a creative inspiration after a break (not after fleeing
fire, run wild or catatonic breakdown).

A pawn can be marked guilty after a destructive break and then be banished or executed with no or smaller
consequences.
