# Dark visions

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: minor mood break, anomalous. DLC: Anomaly.

The pawn wanders around mindlessly, spouting nonsense about things only it can see every 20-60 seconds, even if it cannot talk.

Nearby colonists can get -2 mood from it, stacking up to four times.

On recovery the pawn gets Void catharsis (+30) instead of the normal Catharsis.
