# Entity slayer

Source: RimWorld Wiki, "Mental break" (revision 184203). Not checked in a live game.

Kind: major mood break, anomalous. DLC: Anomaly.

The pawn tries to execute up to 5 random captive entities.

After each kill there is a 50% chance the break ends. At least 625 ticks pass between attempts. It ends early if no fitting entity is left, or after 5 kills.

On recovery the pawn gets Void catharsis (+30) instead of the normal Catharsis.
